Common questions

A few things applicants usually ask before starting their file.

Do I need Navttc certification for every visa?+
No. Navttc trade testing applies to skilled-trade categories specified by the employer or sponsor. We'll confirm whether it applies to your case before booking anything.
Which countries does Gamca cover?+
Gamca medical exams are required for applicants travelling to Gulf Cooperation Council countries. We book the appointment for the specific country and visa category on your file.
What documents do I need to start?+
A valid passport copy, your offer letter or sponsor details, and recent photographs are enough to begin. We'll list anything additional once we review your case.
How do I track my file's status?+
We update you at each stage — appointment booked, biometric done, certificate ready, visa stamped — so you always know where your file stands.
